Sticky Orbs Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Kordius Games, an independent game developer, handles data in the Google Play Android edition of Sticky Orbs and when you contact the developer about the game.

What stays on your device

Sticky Orbs stores game progress and statistics, game currency and skins, unfinished runs, and settings such as sound and controls. If you make a privacy or advertising choice in the app, it stores that choice and the selected age group on your device. These records let you continue playing and keep your preferences. The game does not require an account or send your game profile to a developer server.

Advertising and third-party SDKs

Commercial advertising is disabled in the Google Play configuration covered by this policy. The Android app contains Yandex Mobile Ads and AppMetrica. Automatic initialization of Yandex Mobile Ads is disabled, and the game does not start an independent AppMetrica analytics session or use these SDKs to request commercial ads in this configuration.

When used to serve ads, Yandex Mobile Ads can process device or SDK identifiers, app interactions with ads and technical diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Yandex describes the categories, security and handling for its services in the provider documents below. This policy will be updated before commercial advertising is enabled.

  • Yandex Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Mobile Ads · Data Safety
  • AppMetrica · Data security

What happens when you contact us

If you email Kordius Games, the developer receives your email address, your message and anything you include with it. The developer uses this information to reply, help resolve the issue or handle your data request. The email provider processes the message to deliver and store it. Correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

Storage, protection and deletion

Game data is kept in Android’s private app storage for as long as the app retains the save. You can clear it in Android’s app settings using Clear storage or Clear data. If your version shows Delete local data under Settings → Privacy and data, you can use that option too. Clearing storage removes local progress and preferences from that installation.

Clearing local storage does not remove email correspondence or records held by another provider. You can request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to Kordius Games using the contact email below. For data held by another provider, contact the provider through its privacy policy, or email Kordius Games for help identifying the appropriate channel.

Website and contact

This policy is available on the Kordius Games website hosted by GitHub Pages. The site does not use analytics or advertising trackers. GitHub processes hosting and security information, such as visitors’ IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.
